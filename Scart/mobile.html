Offline First Aid Manual

Locally cached guides available without cellular connection.

1. CPR (Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation)

Place hands in center of chest and push hard and fast (100-120 compressions per minute). Allow chest to rise completely between compressions.

2. Bleeding Management

Apply direct pressure to the wound with a clean cloth. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible to stem bleeding rates.

3. Fractures and Splints

Do not attempt to realign the bone. Immobilize the limb using folded cardboard, sticks, or rolled towels tied securely.

4. Severe Burns

Cool the burn with cold running water for at least 10 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter, or ointments. Wrap loosely with sterile gauze.
